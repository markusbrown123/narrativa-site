## Website design standards

Any page or UI work here follows the `website-standards` skill in `.claude/skills/`: size the job, render it with `playwright-cli` at phone, tablet, and desktop widths, and audit changed files with `web-design-guidelines`. The site's established look (the tokens in `app/globals.css` and the shared components) is the brand; the skills refine it, they don't replace it.
